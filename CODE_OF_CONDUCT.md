# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Origo a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic background, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Be kind, respectful and patient; assume good faith.
- Welcome newcomers and differing viewpoints.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully and focus on what is best for users.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, derogatory comments, or personal/political attacks.
- Trolling, intimidation, doxxing, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Discrimination in any form.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Spam, astroturfing, or deliberately disrupting the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is harmful. Reports can be sent to the maintainer contact in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). All reports will be reviewed and kept confidential to the extent possible.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when an individual represents the project (official accounts, events, mailing lists, forums).

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
